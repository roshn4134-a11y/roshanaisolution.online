The problem

Good proposals can still fail on compliance details.

A 30–80 page solicitation can hide page limits, mandatory forms, amendment acknowledgements, submission mechanics, evaluation factors, and other details that are easy to miss under deadline pressure.

The tool

Built around traceability, review, and practical proposal use.

The browser-based workflow helps turn a solicitation into a reviewable compliance matrix—not a black-box answer.

Full requirement extraction

Pulls compliance requirements from supplied RFP/SOW text and organizes them for review.

Source-traced findings

Findings are tied back to source language so the operator can verify what the solicitation says.

Risk classification

Items can be organized by Critical, High, Medium, or Low review priority.

Document checklist

Required forms, exhibits, attachments, and submission materials are captured in a structured checklist.

Amendment comparison

Amendments can be compared and reviewed so changes are not silently overlooked.

Operator sign-off

Review notes and verification are part of the workflow before a final report is released.

Who it's for

A focused service for teams that need another set of eyes.

Designed around the gap between “we bid regularly” and “we have a full proposal department.”

Good fit

  • IT staffing, consulting, and government services firms
  • Firms bidding RFPs, SOWs, or task orders regularly
  • Teams without a dedicated proposal department
  • State/local GovCon markets such as DIR/ITSAC, 920S, VITA/SWaM, or equivalent

Not a replacement for

  • Legal or procurement counsel
  • The bidder's final compliance responsibility
  • An established enterprise proposal department's internal controls
  • Official instructions from the issuing agency

AI-assisted service intended for human review. It does not replace legal or procurement advice, and final responsibility for compliance remains with the bidder. Official solicitation and amendment documents remain the controlling source.
