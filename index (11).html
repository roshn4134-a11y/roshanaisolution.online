FAQ

Common questions.

What do I need to send?

The solicitation, every amendment, and any attachments or forms, as readable text or PDF files. For the free first check, public solicitation documents are enough.

How long does it take?

The free first check returns the top findings about 48 hours after I receive complete documents. A full review is delivered in 5 business days, and a 48-hour rush can be agreed for urgent deadlines.

Do you use AI?

A software tool does a first pass: it extracts requirements and compares amendments. I then review every item against the source documents, and I sign off the report. A tool can make mistakes, which is why a person checks each finding.

Is my information confidential?

Public solicitation documents are not confidential. Your draft proposal, pricing and internal documents are, and I sign an NDA before reviewing them. Please do not send classified, controlled unclassified or export-controlled material.

Do you guarantee we will pass or win?

No. The agency makes those decisions. What I can do is show you what is missing or inconsistent in the documents so you can fix it before you submit.

Is this legal advice?

No. It is a document review and proposal-readiness service. The official solicitation and amendments control, and your team is responsible for the final submission.
