Frequently asked questions

Before we review your solicitation.

Important details about the service and engagement process.

What is the free first check?

It is a first-pass review of one solicitation and its amendments. I send you the top findings within 48 hours so you can judge the work before deciding on a full review. There is no cost and no obligation. It is not a full review and does not replace one.

Do you use AI or software?

Yes, in part. A tool does the first pass: it extracts requirements and compares amendments. I then review every item against the source documents myself, and the report is reviewed and signed off by me before delivery. The service cannot guarantee that every issue will be found.

What documents should I provide?

Start with the solicitation or RFP, all available attachments, amendments, and any relevant submission instructions. Identify missing files or unreadable scans when you contact us.

Do you review amendments?

Amendment comparison is included when applicable and when the relevant original documents and amendments are supplied. The review identifies changes found in the available materials.

How quickly can you complete a review?

Standard delivery is 5 business days after the complete package is received. A 48-hour rush can be agreed for urgent deadlines, depending on document volume, complexity, readability, and amendment count. The schedule is confirmed before work begins.

Does the review guarantee compliance or a contract award?

No. The service helps identify and organize requirements and potential risks, but it cannot guarantee that every issue will be identified, that a proposal is legally sufficient, or that a bidder will receive an award. Your organization remains responsible for its submission and final decisions.

Is this legal advice?

No. This is a document-review and proposal-readiness service, not legal advice. Consult qualified counsel for legal interpretation, contractual advice, or disputes.

How do I discuss confidentiality?

Contact us before sending sensitive or controlled material. I sign a confidentiality agreement before reviewing anything sensitive. By default the first-pass tool runs locally in my browser and documents are not uploaded to an outside service; any other handling is agreed with you in writing first. We also agree the transfer method and retention terms before you send anything. Do not email classified information or material your organization is not authorized to share.

How do I get started?

Email the solicitation identifier, submission deadline, approximate document volume, and the review you need. Please do not attach sensitive documents until an appropriate transfer method has been agreed.
